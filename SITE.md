# Greylan James — SITE.md

## Project Overview
Official artist website for Greylan James, a Knoxville-raised, Nashville-based country singer-songwriter signed to Big Machine Label Group. This is a rebuild of the existing WordPress site at greylanjames.com, rebranded around his current single "Small Town." The site is a fan hub: hear the music, watch the videos, find tour dates, read his story, and sign up for updates.

## Audience
Country music fans, concertgoers, and industry/press. Mobile first. Most traffic arrives from social posts and streaming links.

## Brand Colors
- Dark Brown #2E2422, primary background, dark sections, header and footer, CMYK 63/68/66/70, RGB 46/36/34, PMS Black C
- Cream #E1DDCE, copy color, headlines and body on dark surfaces, light section background, CMYK 11/9/18/0, RGB 225/221/206, PMS 7527 C
Two colors only. Dark brown is the default background with cream copy. Light sections invert: cream background with dark brown copy. There is no third accent color, so CTAs are a solid fill of the opposite color (cream fill with brown text on dark sections, brown fill with cream text on light sections) and hover states dim rather than shift hue. No other colors. No gradients.

## Textures
Three surface treatments live in the public textures directory, generated by scripts/generate-textures.mjs and applied with the Texture component. Re-run the script after editing it; the output is deterministic.
- press, used on the home Music section. Newsprint dot screen over letterpress paper tooth. Reads as printed matter.
- tape, used on the home Videos section. Scanlines, tape dust, and dropout streaks over the same grain. The only variant with scanlines, because it is the section that is about video.
- paper, used on the home About and site index sections. Coarse newsprint tooth only, so nothing competes with body copy.
A fourth and fifth tile, tear-top and tear-bottom, are masks rather than artwork, used by the TornEdge component. Where a brown section meets a photograph, the brown tears over the image instead of ending on a ruled line, the way a poster comes off a wall. Currently used at three boundaries on the home page: the bottom of the hero, and the top and bottom of the tour section. Put TornEdge on the brown section and point it at the photo, never the other way around, and do not clip the host section's overflow.

Every tile is drawn in cream and dark brown with a varying alpha, so textures never introduce a third color. They are composited normally rather than with a blend mode, because the dark brown surface is too dark for multiply, overlay, or soft-light to show. Textures sit behind section content, never over album art or video players, and are inert: aria-hidden and non-interactive. They are fully static, with no animated tracking lines, so there is nothing for prefers-reduced-motion to suppress.

## Typography
- Headlines and sub-heads: Knockout HTF67 Full Bantamweight, Regular. Self-hosted, font file is in the public fonts directory. Uppercase, tight, condensed. Used for hero, section titles, and large stacked navigation type.
- Body copy: Acumin Pro Condensed, Regular.
- Headline fallback stack: "Knockout HTF67 Full Bantamweight", "Arial Narrow", sans-serif.
- Body fallback stack: "Acumin Pro Condensed", "Arial Narrow", system-ui, sans-serif.
Headlines are uppercase and condensed. Body is sentence case.

## Logo
Logo files are in the public logos directory: GreylanJames_LogoColored.png (cream script wordmark, two lines, with the Tennessee state outline as the dot detail), GreylanJames_LogoInitials.png (cream G monogram with stars), GreylanJames_LogoWhite.png (pure white wordmark), and greyland-james-favicon.png (the G monogram inside a double-ruled circle, on black). There is currently no dark brown logo file for use on cream sections. Request one from the client if a logo needs to appear on a light section.

Everything in public/ that is derived rather than supplied is built by `node scripts/generate-icons.mjs`. The favicon roundel produces favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon.png, and both android-chrome sizes. The OG card is the home hero photo with the colored wordmark composited low, so it clears Greylan's face, over a bottom-weighted scrim that keeps the cream letters legible against his light shirt. Rerun the script after changing either source file; do not hand-edit the generated files.

## Tone of Voice
Plainspoken, grounded, country. Short declarative sentences. Confident but not boastful. Warm and human in body copy, punchy and bold in headlines. No corporate or marketing hype words. No clichés. No em dashes anywhere in site copy.

## Art Direction and Visual References
Gritty rural Americana. Overcast, film grain, lived in, documentary feel. Not glossy or glamorous. Anchored to three references:
1. Vintage athletic and wood-type signage, the heritage of the Knockout typeface. Bold condensed caps, scoreboard and ticket-stub energy. The Small Town cover art features a roadside scoreboard sign; lean into that.
2. Documentary Appalachian photography. Rural Tennessee, barns, back roads, natural overcast light, film grain.
3. Newsprint and classified-ad layout. Cream paper stock, heavy dark headlines, no accent color. Editorial and utilitarian.
Hard edges over rounded corners. Square buttons, never pills. Large condensed type used as a graphic element.

## Design Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- No pill buttons and no gradient buttons. Use hard-edged square buttons.
- No icons inside colored circles.
- No three-column service or feature card grids.
- No centered hero with a feature grid below.
- No blue or teal palettes.
- No stock photography of smiling people.
- No rounded glossy UI. Keep it raw, editorial, and high contrast.
